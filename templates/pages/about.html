01 / ABOUT THE ORGANISATION

Who we are

The Storybook World is a subsidiary of Superkids World, a social, education and entertainment outfit that promotes novelty, talent and morals, equipping children with the necessary skills, abilities and competencies to develop society and become global champions.

At The Storybook World, we celebrate the magic of storybooks and craft engaging experiences for young readers. We use knowledge and values-based stories across different genres, including African folktales, mystery books, feature books and biographies, to inspire children of Africa.

02 / OUR CAMPAIGN

Superkids-Are-Inspired-by-SuperStorybook Campaign

The erosion of values among children poses a threat to our future and leadership recruitment process. Concerned about this attitudinal decline and societal disorderliness, our organisation initiated the Superkids-Are-Inspired-by-SuperStorybook Campaign and the launch of the Superkids Storybook Club in primary and secondary schools across Nigeria.

The campaign is designed to create awareness of the advantages of reading and using stories to impact morals in Nigeria's future leaders.

The campaign launch brings book writers, book reviewers and students together in an atmosphere of fun, adventures and refreshments. Students are also introduced to how they can package and fund their literary content into books, while the Superkids Storybook Club is launched.

Inspiring young minds through stories and reading.

03 / THE COMMUNITY

The Superkids Storybook Club.

The Superkids Storybook Club is a community of Nigerian children inspired by the power of stories and books to help reshape and redefine leadership.

Members are dedicated to exploring the world of storytelling and fostering a lifelong love for reading and knowledge as tools for social change.
